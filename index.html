About the Firm

Practical accounting, built on close attention to detail

Seshapu Bala Manikanta & Co is a Chartered Accountant practice based in Eluru, working with proprietors, partnership firms, private companies, and individual taxpayers across the district.

The firm's focus is on the work that has the most bearing on a business's financial standing — accurate income tax filing, tax audit compliance, and the loan projections and CMA data that banks require for working capital and term loans.

Alongside this, the firm handles GST filing, bookkeeping, and statutory audit for businesses that need ongoing compliance support, including agri-processing and export-oriented units common to this region.
